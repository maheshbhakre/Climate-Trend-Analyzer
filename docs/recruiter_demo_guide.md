# Recruiter Demo Guide

## Five-second opening

Open **Executive Overview**. Point to the four KPI cards, the annual signal, the derived warming slope, and the operational status row. The story is immediately visible: this is a working intelligence product, not a static chart.

## Suggested 90-second flow

1. **Executive Overview:** show quality, alert volume, and the traceable summary.
2. **Global Climate Trends:** switch between trend, distribution, and decade lens to demonstrate a coherent Plotly system.
3. **Country Explorer:** select a country and show the same data becoming an actionable location view.
4. **Climate Alert Center:** filter to severe/extreme events and search a city.
5. **Forecast Lab:** select city level, choose a 24- or 36-month horizon, and call out the historical/forecast/confidence-band distinction.
6. **Data Quality Center:** close on completeness, coverage, schema checks, and the quality score.

## What to say

“The presentation layer is decoupled from the validated engines. Every executive insight is derived from existing reports, while the dashboard adds caching, an enterprise visual system, responsive interaction, and a recruiter-friendly narrative.”

## Strong proof points

- No fabricated climate signals: insights are generated from report values.
- Forecast views expose model, horizon, confidence bands, and future risk.
- Alert operations include severity, country, city search, and ranked risk.
- Data quality and methodology are first-class product surfaces.
